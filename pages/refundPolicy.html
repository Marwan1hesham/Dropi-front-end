Refund Policy

Easy refunds, no stress.

We want your Dropi experience to feel confident and fair. If a product doesn’t match your expectations, our refund process is designed to be simple and fast.

How refunds work

Refund requests are accepted within 14 days of delivery. Items must be returned in their original condition with all packaging intact.

  • Submit your refund request using our support email.
  • Return the item in original packaging within 14 days.
  • Once received, we process refunds within 5-7 business days.

If your item is damaged, incorrect, or not as described, we’ll help you with a full refund or replacement.

Need help?

Reach out to our support team for fast assistance and return instructions.
